About Us

Diverse wellness and lifestyle supply, reliably delivered.

SEEDORA LIMITED is a Mauritius-based company engaged in the wholesale trade of wellness, lifestyle, and general consumer products. The product scope is international trade in healthcare and wellness products that do not require medical or pharmaceutical licensing.

We work with suppliers and partners to provide access to a diverse product range across different categories. The company supports clients with reliable supply solutions tailored to different business needs.

Our approach: flexibility, structured cooperation, and long-term business relationships.
